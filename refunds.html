Refund and cancellation policy

Free homepage preview

Website orders start with a free preview of your homepage. You don't pay anything to see it, and you owe nothing if you decide not to continue. Previews stay open for 14 days.

Payment to continue

If you continue after your preview, you pay the full price before we build the rest of your site. For Systems, you pay half then and the rest when you approve. That payment is fully refundable until we begin work on the rest of your site. Once work has started, it's refundable only for the part we haven't done.

Deposits for other work

Design and setup work without a website starts with a 20% deposit, and we don't start any work until it's paid. Your deposit is fully refundable until we begin work on your project. Once work has started, it's refundable only for the part of the work we haven't done. If you pay in full up front, the same rule applies to the whole amount.

Final payment

You review your work on a private preview first. Once you approve it in writing, anything still due is paid before your site or system goes live, and before we connect your web address or hand anything over. Finished and approved work isn't refundable.

Web address setup

If we buy a web address for you, the $99 setup fee and the web address cost aren't refundable once it's purchased. You'll see both on your quote first. It's registered in your name, and yearly renewals are yours.

Printed welcome folder

The $99 folder, including shipping, is refundable until we send it to print.

Care plans

Care plans are billed monthly. You can cancel any time, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month. We don't refund partial months. If your plan ends, we'll give you 30 days' notice and send your site files before taking your site offline. To cancel, use your billing page or submit a ticket on our support page.

VFM Engine

VFM Engine is billed monthly. You can cancel any time on your billing page or by submitting a ticket on our support page, and you won't be charged again after that. Past months aren't refunded, and results aren't guaranteed.

Discount codes

VFM NOVA's own codes give 30% off your setup price, up to $750 off. Affiliate codes give 15% off your setup price. Code HOLIDAY gives new customers 15% off your setup price through 11:59 pm Pacific on January 1, 2027. You can use one code per project, and codes can't be combined with each other or other offers. Each code also takes the same percent off VFM Engine for as long as the plan stays active. Unless a code's own terms say otherwise, they don't apply to care plans, web address setup or the printed folder. Codes have no cash value and are one per business.
