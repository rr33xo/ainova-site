Quick fixes

Try these before you book

A few common problems have simple fixes. If none of these help, book a visit and we'll take it from there.

Check the thermostat

Make sure it's set to Heat or Cool, the temperature is set past the room temperature, and the batteries aren't dead.

Look at the breaker

Find the breakers for your furnace or air handler and the outdoor unit. If one is tripped, reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and book a visit.

Change a dirty filter

A clogged filter can cut airflow and even shut your system down. Most filters need changing every one to three months.

Clear the outdoor unit

Leaves, snow or plants around the outdoor unit block airflow. Keep about two feet of space around it.

Open the vents

Closed or blocked vents can make rooms uneven and strain your system. Keep furniture and rugs off them.

Tune-ups

Twice a year keeps breakdowns away

A tune-up before summer and another before winter helps your system run efficiently and catches problems early.

  • Check and clean key parts
  • Test safety controls and electrical connections
  • Check refrigerant lines and drains
  • Replace or check your filter
  • Make sure your thermostat is reading right
