Straight talk

Where do my posts appear?

On VFM's own social channels, clearly labeled as ads. We show you the current channels and their audience before you pay, so you can decide if it's right for you. Your search pages and directory listing live on our website.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We put your business in front of more people, build pages for search and report what happened. We don't promise a set amount of traffic, sales or rankings.

Does every business qualify?

No. We review each business first. We can't promote adult, gambling, financial-advice or health-claim products, or anything that breaks platform rules.

What do I need to join?

A live website or store, and the products or services you want promoted. Don't have a website? Add the Engine to one of our packages.

Can I use a discount code?

Yes. Enter your code when you sign up, and it comes off your monthly price for as long as your plan stays active.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, and you won't be charged again. Past months aren't refunded. See our refund policy.
