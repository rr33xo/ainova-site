About us

It started with Biscuit

Paw Pals began with my rescue, Biscuit, who shook at every groomer we tried. So I learned to do it myself, slowly, with treats and breaks and a lot of praise. Now every pup gets the Biscuit treatment: one dog at a time, at their pace, and a text with a photo the moment they're ready.
